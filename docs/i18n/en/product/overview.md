# Product Overview

Brk-Trkr is a LEGO collection and inventory management platform intended to connect catalog truth, owned assets, loose inventory, build/project planning, storage, transactions, and valuation in one coherent system.

## Product goals

Brk-Trkr is designed to help users answer questions such as:

- What sets, parts, minifigures, colors, themes, and related catalog entities exist?
- Which physical assets do I own, and what is their condition and lifecycle state?
- What loose inventory is available, reserved, missing, or allocated?
- What parts are required for a build or project, and what can be satisfied from inventory?
- Where are physical items stored?
- What did an item cost, how was it acquired or disposed of, and what is it worth now?
- Which items are wanted, missing, active, retired, or otherwise relevant to a collection workflow?

## Core product distinction

The platform separates **catalog identity** from **physical ownership and possession**. A catalog entity describes what an item *is*; an owned asset or inventory position describes what a user or organization *has*.

That distinction is the foundation for the domain model documented in [Architecture → Domain Architecture](../architecture/domains/README.md).
