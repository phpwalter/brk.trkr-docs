# Glossary

## Catalog entity
A canonical definition of a LEGO-related entity independent of ownership. Examples include sets, parts, minifigures, colors, themes, and categories.

## Owned asset
A specific physical instance owned by a user or organization. It may reference a catalog entity but has its own lifecycle, condition, provenance, and financial history.

## Inventory position
A tracked quantity or discrete stock position available to a collection or organizational context.

## Collection
A logical view or grouping used to organize owned assets and inventory. A collection is not necessarily equivalent to a physical storage container.

## Reservation
An allocation of available inventory to a build, project, order, or other consumer.

## Bill of materials (BOM)
The required parts and quantities for a build or project.

## Acquisition
A transaction that brings an asset or inventory into ownership or possession.

## Disposal
A transaction that removes an asset or inventory from ownership or possession, such as a sale, transfer, loss, or retirement.

## Location
A physical or logical storage position used to identify where an item can be found.

## Valuation
An estimated current monetary value derived from market data and relevant item attributes such as condition and completeness.

## ADR
Architecture Decision Record: a durable record of a significant architectural decision, its context, alternatives, and consequences.
