# Future / Research Architecture

The source archive contains advanced architectural proposals that are **not established as current Brk-Trkr architecture**. They are retained as research and ADR input rather than authoritative requirements.

## Research themes from the archive

- Marketplace and channel matrix
- Shipping pipelines and cross-border logistics
- Team topology
- Predictive ML and inference pipelines
- Hardware automation, AMR dispatch, sensor fusion, and IoT/digital twins
- Digital guides
- Regulatory and data-sovereignty concerns
- Disaster recovery and archival strategies
- Cache optimization
- GraphQL federation
- Chaos engineering
- Business intelligence
- Zero-knowledge provenance and provenance ledgers
- Graph topology and spatial trees
- Event sourcing
- Distributed locking and distributed transactions
- Spatial optimization, collision, pathfinding, and mesh simplification
- Telemetry streams
- Storage sharding and global replication
- Live synchronization and edge-mesh architectures
- Zero-trust infrastructure
- Tenant billing
- Predictive resupply and predictive maintenance
- Monorepo synthesis

## Promotion criteria

A topic should move into authoritative architecture only when:

1. a current product requirement depends on it;
2. the chosen design has been validated against the current frontend/API/database architecture;
3. operational cost and complexity are justified; and
4. significant trade-offs are captured in an ADR.
